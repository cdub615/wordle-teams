The whole thing, start to finish

1

Make a team

Invite the people you already send your score to every morning — by email address, or just by sharing a join link. Two teams are free. If somebody already invited you, you are on their team and it is waiting for your first score.

 
2

Enter your board

Play Wordle wherever you normally do, then type the result in. The board fills as you type and backspace goes back a letter, so it is about ten seconds and you never leave the keyboard.

 
3

Scores settle

The table fills in as everyone plays, the month adds up, and somebody wins it. There is a team chat for arguing about the word, and a reminder at a time you pick if you would rather not have to remember.
